- “Game gun shot” — https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/1662/1662-preview.mp3
- “Shotgun hard pump” — https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/1665/1665-preview.mp3
- “Exclamation of pain from a zombie” — https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/2207/2207-preview.mp3
- “Explosion with rocks debris” — https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/1703/1703-preview.mp3
- “Martial arts kick” — https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/2163/2163-preview.mp3

Edits include trimming, mono conversion, EQ, loudness normalization, pitch adjustment, fading, and mixing to create weapon-specific variants.
